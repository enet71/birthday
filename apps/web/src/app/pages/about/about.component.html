Your birthday calendar

Birthdly is a free birthday calendar. It opens straight to your calendar, so you can see whose birthday is coming up and add new ones in seconds.

  • See every birthday on a month or year calendar.
  • Check upcoming celebrations and how many days are left.
  • Find people quickly with search.
  • Sign in with Google to keep your birthdays in your account on every device.

Frequently asked questions

Is Birthdly free?

Yes. Birthdly is free to use.

 Do I need an account?

No. You can add birthdays right away without signing in. Sign in with Google when you want to keep them in your account.

 Where are my birthdays stored?

Without an account, birthdays are saved in this browser only. After you sign in with Google, they are stored in your account, and the birthdays you added before signing in can be moved there.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Birthdly works in any modern mobile browser, and you can add it to your home screen to open it like an app.

 How do I add a birthday?

Open the calendar, press the + button, and enter a name and a date.
