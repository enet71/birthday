How it works

  1. Open the calendar

    Birthdly opens straight to your calendar. There is nothing to install.

  2. Add birthdays

    Press the + button and enter a name and a date. The birth year is optional.

  3. Sync if you like

    Sign in with Google to keep birthdays on every device, turn on birthday notifications, and connect Google Calendar.
