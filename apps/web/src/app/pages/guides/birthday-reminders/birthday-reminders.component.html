Most forgotten birthdays are not forgotten at all: you simply did not think of the date in time. A reminder the day before solves that, and it works best when it arrives on the phone you already carry. This guide shows how to get birthday reminders with Birthdly and Google Calendar.

What makes a good birthday reminder

  • It comes early enough. The day before leaves time to send a card, order a gift, or plan a call.
  • It comes where you look. A notification on your phone works better than an app you have to open.
  • It repeats every year on its own. You set up a birthday once and never touch it again.
  • It is easy to keep complete. Reminders only help for the people you actually added.

How reminders work with Birthdly

Birthdly keeps your list of birthdays, and Google Calendar delivers the reminders. Birthdly does not send notifications or emails on its own. Instead, it adds each birthday to your Google Calendar as a birthday event with a reminder at 9:00 the day before. Google Calendar then shows that reminder on your phone, tablet, and computer, wherever you are signed in to Google.

This way you get reminders in an app you already use every day, and you do not need yet another app that asks for notification permissions.

Set up birthday reminders step by step

  1. Add birthdays. Open the calendar, press Add birthday, and enter a name and a date. The birth year is optional.
  2. Sign in with Google using the account button in the top right corner.
  3. Connect Google Calendar. Open Settings and press Connect Google Calendar. Birthdly creates a birthday event with a reminder for every birthday in your list, except the birthdays that come from your Google Contacts.
  4. Allow notifications for Google Calendar. On your phone, make sure notifications are turned on for the Google Calendar app. On a computer, allow notifications for Google Calendar in your browser.

That is all. From now on, every birthday you add or change in Birthdly gets its event and reminder in Google Calendar as well. Birthdays from your Google Contacts are the exception, as the next sections explain. The Google Calendar guide explains the sync in more detail.

Keep upcoming birthdays in view

A reminder covers the day before. For a longer look ahead, use Birthdly itself:

  • The Upcoming panel next to the calendar shows the next birthdays with labels such as "Tomorrow" or "in 5 days", and how old each person turns.
  • The year view shows all birthdays of the year at once, which helps when you plan presents or trips.
  • The People page lists everyone in one table that you can sort and search.

Use the notes of a birthday for gift ideas. When the reminder arrives, open the person in Birthdly and the ideas are right there.

Birthdays from Google Contacts

Google Calendar already shows the birthdays saved in your Google Contacts. Birthdly brings those birthdays into its own list too, but it does not add a second event for them, so you do not get two reminders. Reminders for these birthdays come from Google Calendar itself and follow its default notification settings for all-day events, so check those settings if you want a reminder for them too.

Tips for never missing a birthday

  • Add birthdays as soon as you hear them, even without the year. You can fill in the year later.
  • Save the relationship, such as family, friends, or colleagues, so you can see at a glance who is who.
  • Once a month, open the year view and check that nobody important is missing.

Frequently asked questions

Can Birthdly send me email reminders?

Not at the moment. Reminders come from Google Calendar after you connect it.

When does the reminder arrive?

For birthdays you add in Birthdly, at 9:00 the day before the birthday, in the time zone of your Google Calendar. For birthdays from your Google Contacts, it depends on your Google Calendar notification settings.

Do I get reminders without connecting Google Calendar?

No. Without Google Calendar you can still see upcoming birthdays in Birthdly, but nothing notifies you. Connect Google Calendar to get reminders.
