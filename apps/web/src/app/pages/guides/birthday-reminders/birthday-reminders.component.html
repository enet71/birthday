Most forgotten birthdays are not forgotten at all: you simply did not think of the date in time. A reminder the day before solves that, and it works best when it arrives on the device you already use. This guide shows how to get birthday reminders with Birthdly: as notifications from Birthdly itself, through Google Calendar, or both.

What makes a good birthday reminder

  • It comes early enough. The day before leaves time to send a card, order a gift, or plan a call.
  • It comes where you look. A notification on your phone or computer works better than an app you have to open.
  • It repeats every year on its own. You set up a birthday once and never touch it again.
  • It is easy to keep complete. Reminders only help for the people you actually added.

How reminders work with Birthdly

Birthdly keeps your list of birthdays and can remind you about them in two ways:

  • Birthday notifications. Birthdly sends a browser notification with the birthdays of the day to every device where you turn them on. You choose whether it comes on the day, the day before, or both, and at what time. The notification arrives even when Birthdly is closed.
  • Google Calendar. Birthdly adds each birthday to your Google Calendar as a birthday event with a reminder at 9:00 the day before. Google Calendar then shows that reminder wherever you are signed in to Google.

Both need you to sign in with Google. You can use either one, or both at the same time.

Turn on birthday notifications

  1. Add birthdays. Open the calendar, press Add birthday, and enter a name and a date. The birth year is optional.
  2. Sign in with Google using the account button in the top right corner.
  3. Turn on notifications. Open Settings, find Birthday notifications, and press Turn on for this device. When your browser asks, allow notifications.
  4. Choose when. Pick on the day, the day before, or both, and the time. These settings apply to all your devices. The time is in the time zone of the device where you last turned notifications on or changed these settings, shown under the controls.

Notifications are turned on for each device separately, so repeat steps 2 and 3 on your phone and your computer. They cover every birthday in your account, including the ones imported from Google Contacts. Birthdays on the same day come together in one notification.

On iPhone and iPad, notifications need iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later. Add Birthdly to your Home Screen first: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open Birthdly from the Home Screen icon and turn on notifications there.

If you blocked notifications for Birthdly earlier, the button does not appear. Allow them in your browser's site settings for Birthdly, then reload the page.

Get reminders in Google Calendar

  1. Add birthdays and sign in with Google, as described above.
  2. Connect Google Calendar. Open Settings and press Connect Google Calendar. Birthdly creates a birthday event with a reminder for every birthday in your list, except the birthdays that come from your Google Contacts.
  3. Allow notifications for Google Calendar. On your phone, make sure notifications are turned on for the Google Calendar app. On a computer, allow notifications for Google Calendar in your browser.

From now on, every birthday you add or change in Birthdly gets its event and reminder in Google Calendar as well. Birthdays from your Google Contacts are the exception, as the next sections explain. The Google Calendar guide explains the sync in more detail.

Keep upcoming birthdays in view

A reminder covers the next day or two. For a longer look ahead, use Birthdly itself:

  • The Upcoming panel next to the calendar shows the next birthdays with labels such as "Tomorrow" or "in 5 days", and how old each person turns.
  • The year view shows all birthdays of the year at once, which helps when you plan presents or trips.
  • The People page lists everyone in one table that you can sort and search.

Use the notes of a birthday for gift ideas. When the reminder arrives, open the person in Birthdly and the ideas are right there.

Birthdays from Google Contacts

Birthday notifications from Birthdly include the birthdays imported from your Google Contacts, like any other birthday in your account.

Google Calendar already shows the birthdays saved in your Google Contacts, so Birthdly does not add a second event for them, and you do not get two reminders from Google Calendar. Its reminders for these birthdays follow its default notification settings for all-day events, so check those settings if you want a reminder for them too.

Tips for never missing a birthday

  • Add birthdays as soon as you hear them, even without the year. You can fill in the year later.
  • Save the relationship, such as family, friends, or colleagues, so you can see at a glance who is who.
  • Once a month, open the year view and check that nobody important is missing.

Frequently asked questions

Can Birthdly send me email reminders?

Not at the moment. Birthdly sends browser notifications, and Google Calendar can remind you after you connect it.

When does the reminder arrive?

Birthday notifications arrive at the time you choose in Settings, 9:00 unless you change it, on the day, the day before, or both. Google Calendar reminders for birthdays you add in Birthdly arrive at 9:00 the day before, in the time zone of your Google Calendar. For birthdays from your Google Contacts, Google Calendar follows its own notification settings.

Do I get reminders without connecting Google Calendar?

Yes. Turn on birthday notifications in Settings. They do not depend on Google Calendar.

Do notifications work when Birthdly is closed?

Yes. You do not need to keep Birthdly open. On a computer, the browser itself needs to be running. If it is not, the notification arrives when you open the browser later that day.

Will I get the same birthday twice?

With On the day and the day before, the default, you hear about each birthday twice: the day before and on the day. Pick one of them in Settings if you want one notification. If you also connected Google Calendar, its reminder comes on top; turn off the reminder in Google Calendar, or turn off notifications for a device in Settings, if that is too much.
