Google Calendar is where many people already plan their week, so it is a natural place for birthdays too. This guide shows two ways to get birthdays into Google Calendar: by hand in Google, and automatically with Birthdly, which keeps them in sync for you.

How Google Calendar shows birthdays

Google Calendar has a special kind of event for birthdays. It is an all-day event that repeats every year and appears with a cake icon. Google creates these events on its own for the people in your Google Contacts who have a birthday saved, so if a friend's birthday is in their contact card, you will usually see it in your calendar already.

For everyone else, such as colleagues, neighbors, or relatives you never saved as contacts, Google Calendar shows nothing until you add the birthday yourself.

Option 1: add birthdays in Google

There are two common ways to do it without any extra tools.

Add the birthday to a contact

  1. Open Google Contacts and find the person, or create a new contact.
  2. Choose Edit, add the Birthday field, and enter the date. The year is optional.
  3. Save the contact. The birthday appears in Google Calendar as a yearly birthday event.

Create a yearly event

  1. In Google Calendar, create an all-day event on the birthday with the person's name as the title.
  2. Set it to repeat annually and add a notification, for example one day before.
  3. Save the event.

Both work, but they get tedious with more than a handful of people. Contacts fill up with entries you only need for a date, and repeating events are easy to get wrong, especially for someone born on February 29.

Option 2: sync birthdays from Birthdly

Birthdly is a birthday calendar made for this job. You keep all birthdays in one list, with the relationship and notes such as gift ideas, and Birthdly adds them to Google Calendar as real birthday events.

  1. Add your birthdays. Open the calendar, press Add birthday, and enter a name and a date. You can leave the birth year empty.
  2. Sign in with Google. Use the account button in the top right corner. Birthdays you added before signing in can be moved to your account.
  3. Connect Google Calendar. Open Settings, find the Google Calendar card, and press Connect Google Calendar. Google shows two permissions: to see, create, change, and delete events on calendars you own, and to see your contacts. Birthdly only changes the birthday events it creates and never changes your contacts.
  4. Done. Your birthdays appear in your primary Google calendar within moments.

Birthdly reads your contacts only to bring their birthdays into Birthdly and never changes them. In Google Calendar it changes only the birthday events it created itself.

What the birthday events look like

  • Each birthday is a Google birthday event that repeats every year, so it shows up just like the birthdays of your contacts.
  • Every event has a reminder at 9:00 the day before, which gives you time to call or buy a present.
  • Events are private and do not mark you as busy, so they never block meetings.
  • A birthday on February 29 appears on February 28 in other years.
  • People whose birthday comes from your Google Contacts do not get a second event, because Google already shows their birthday. You still see them in Birthdly.

Keeping Birthdly and Google Calendar in sync

Once connected, you can keep working in Birthdly and let it update Google:

  • When you add a birthday in Birthdly or change its name or date, its event in Google Calendar changes too.
  • When you delete a birthday in Birthdly, its event disappears from Google Calendar.
  • When you delete one of Birthdly's birthday events in Google Calendar, Birthdly deletes that birthday as well.
  • Birthdly checks Google for changes when you open the calendar, at most once an hour. To check right away, press Sync now in Settings.

Google does not allow other edits to birthday events, such as changing the date in Google Calendar. Make those changes in Birthdly instead.

How to stop syncing

Open Settings and press Disconnect on the Google Calendar card. Birthdly removes the birthday events it added to Google Calendar, and your birthdays stay in Birthdly. You can connect again at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Birthdly account?

You can add birthdays without an account. To sync with Google Calendar, sign in with Google, because the sync runs for your account on every device.

Which calendar do the birthdays go to?

Your primary Google calendar, the one named after your Google account.

Will I see duplicates of my contacts' birthdays?

Not when the name and birthday match. If a person has the same name and birthday in Birthdly and in Google Contacts, Birthdly links them instead of adding the person twice, and it does not create a second event. If the names differ, for example "Mom" in Birthdly and her full name in Google Contacts, you will see both. In that case, delete the one you added by hand in Birthdly.
