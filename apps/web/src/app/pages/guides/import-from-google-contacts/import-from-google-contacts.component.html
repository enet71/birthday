If you use Android or Gmail, many birthdays are probably already saved in your Google Contacts. Instead of typing them again, you can bring them into Birthdly in one step and keep them up to date automatically. This guide explains how the import works and what happens to your contacts.

Before you start

  • You need a Google account with contacts that have a birthday saved.
  • Only contacts saved in your Google Contacts are imported. People you only emailed, which Google lists under "Other contacts", are not included.
  • A contact needs a name and at least the day and month of the birthday. The year is optional.

Import birthdays step by step

  1. Open Birthdly and sign in with Google using the account button in the top right corner.
  2. Open Settings and find the Google Calendar card.
  3. Press Connect Google Calendar. Google shows two permissions: to see, create, change, and delete events on calendars you own, and to see your contacts. Birthdly only changes the birthday events it creates and never changes your contacts. Allow both.
  4. Birthdly starts the first sync right away. After a short while, the birthdays of your contacts appear in the calendar and on the People page.

The import comes with the Google Calendar connection, so the same step also adds your Birthdly birthdays to Google Calendar with a reminder the day before.

What happens to your contacts

Nothing. Birthdly only reads your contacts and never changes, adds, or deletes them. It reads the name and birthday of each contact and ignores everything else, such as phone numbers and email addresses.

How duplicates are avoided

If you added someone in Birthdly before importing, Birthdly tries to match them with your contacts. A person is matched when the name and the day and month of the birthday are the same and the birth years do not conflict; a missing year always matches, and letter case and extra spaces do not matter. A matched person stays one entry in Birthdly and keeps the relationship and notes you saved.

If the names differ, for example "Mom" in Birthdly and her full name in Google Contacts, Birthdly cannot tell they are the same person and you see two entries. Delete the one you added by hand in Birthdly.

Keeping imported birthdays up to date

Birthdly checks your contacts again when you open the calendar, at most once an hour, and when you press Sync now in Settings.

  • New contacts with a birthday are added to Birthdly.
  • A birthday or name changed in Google Contacts is updated in Birthdly, unless you edited that person in Birthdly more recently. Your latest edit wins.
  • A contact without a birth year never erases a year you already saved in Birthdly.
  • A birthday removed from a contact stays in Birthdly. From then on, Birthdly adds it to Google Calendar as its own birthday event.

Removing an imported birthday

While a contact still has a birthday in Google Contacts, deleting it in Birthdly does not last: the next sync imports it again. To remove such a birthday for good, first remove the birthday from the contact in Google Contacts, then delete it in Birthdly.

Imported birthdays in Google Calendar

Google Calendar already shows the birthdays of your contacts, so Birthdly does not add a second event for them. You see each contact's birthday once. Its reminder comes from Google Calendar and follows your default notification settings for all-day events. The birthday reminders guide explains how reminders work.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import birthdays without connecting Google Calendar?

No. The import is part of the Google Calendar connection.

What happens to imported birthdays if I disconnect?

They stay in Birthdly. Disconnecting only removes the events Birthdly added to Google Calendar and stops the sync.

Can I add notes to imported birthdays?

Yes. Imported birthdays work like any other: you can set the relationship and add notes, such as gift ideas.
